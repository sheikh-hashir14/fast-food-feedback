How it works

Shared, item-level reviews

Every review is stored in the campus database, so what you write is what the next student sees. Ratings are for a single item, not for a whole outlet.

One review per item per device

Your browser creates a random device ID. It stops accidental double posting and keeps the averages honest. It is not a login system, so it reduces spam rather than eliminating it.

Prices are student-reported

Outlet names, menu items and prices in this demo are placeholders. Check the printed menu at the counter before you trust a price.
